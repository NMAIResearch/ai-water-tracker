Method and guardrails

WCI. WCI = (average withdrawal / host utility capacity) × consumptive ratio × peaking factor. A value is a pressure index, not a volume and not a water-quality score.

Consumption. Average consumption is withdrawal multiplied by the consumptive ratio. Ratios for non-Google rows are assigned framework defaults rather than site measurements.

Off-site share. This is a grid-average modelled band calculated from cooling WUE and the regional electricity-water intensity band. It is not a metered per-site value.

Source labels. Unknown means the current ledger does not support a classification. A reservoir in the hydropower field describes an electricity coupling and is not evidence of the site's cooling-water source.

Denominator. The seed remains ten rows: six primary-verified rows, three planning rows and one framework-secondary row. Filters change the display, never the denominator.

Reproducibility. build_tool.py embeds sites.csv and the conservative water_source_evidence.csv overlay into this file. The page makes no network request after loading.
